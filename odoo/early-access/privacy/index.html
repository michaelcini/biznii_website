BIZNII FOR ODOO / EARLY ACCESS PRIVACY

Early Access Privacy Notice

Your application, your choices.

Registration is closed while we verify the submission-provider settings. The controller identity and three-month registration retention period below have been confirmed by the owner. This notice does not replace the existing app privacy policy.

Who is responsible

The data controller for this programme is Michael Cini, operating under the BizNii brand from Malta. For privacy enquiries, requests or withdrawal of consent, contact support@biznii.com.

What we collect and why

Your email address is required so we can manage your application and contact you about registration and testing opportunities. Odoo edition, Odoo version, Android version, interest and comments are optional. These help us understand compatibility needs, review requested features and select testers manually. We also keep the provider’s receipt date, consent answer, privacy-notice version and a private application status.

Please do not submit credentials, API keys, database exports or private business information. We do not need your name, phone number, business address or Odoo server details. The submission provider may process connection metadata such as IP address and browser information for delivery and abuse prevention; its confirmed arrangements must be described before launch.

Legal basis and consent

The programme uses consent under GDPR Article 6(1)(a) as its legal basis. The registration checkbox is unchecked by default. Registration will require your affirmative consent to the stated application purposes. You may leave every technical question blank. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing support@biznii.com; withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal. Without this consent, we cannot manage an application.

Storage, recipients and international transfers

No applications are currently collected. The selected submission provider is Formspark, operated by Trampoline Software SRL in Belgium. Its published Data Processing Agreement describes storage in Ireland and Germany, receipt of connection metadata and processing on the controller’s behalf. Its subprocessor list describes EEA hosting and some processing outside the EEA, with safeguards including adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Before launch, the owner must review these arrangements and identify any notification-email or private tracking providers used. The programme submission endpoint has been configured, but registration remains closed while the owner reviews the account settings, provider arrangements and this notice.

Applications will be accessible only to authorised BizNii administrators and contracted processors involved in registration. Applicant records will not be placed in the public website or GitHub repository. Google Play will not receive your registration automatically; any later Play invitation or code redemption has separate Google processing.

Retention and deletion

Registration records will be deleted no later than three months after receipt, or earlier following withdrawal or a valid deletion request where applicable. This covers the application in Formspark and copies held in exports, private status trackers and any notification emails. BizNii manages deletion of these records. Any longer tester participation will need its own information and appropriate legal basis.

The owner must apply deletion to the provider, notification emails, exports and any private status tracker. Formspark’s published DPA currently specifies 30-day recovery for deleted submissions, 12 months for submitter IP addresses and derived location, 12 months for filtered submissions (visible for 30 days), and seven days for provider-generated export files. These processor periods are separate from BizNii’s three-month registration retention period; deleting an application does not immediately erase all provider logs or recoverable copies. Provider settings and arrangements remain subject to review before launch. Registrations must not be retained indefinitely.

Your rights and privacy enquiries

Email support@biznii.com to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability where applicable, or withdrawal of consent. Use the address you registered with so we can verify your request without asking for unnecessary identity documents. You may also have a right to object where applicable. You can complain to Malta’s Information and Data Protection Commissioner or your local supervisory authority.

Marketing and tester selection

Registration is not a newsletter subscription. No broader marketing consent is requested. Choosing “future updates” does not authorise unrelated marketing. Any future newsletter would require a separate optional choice. Selection is manual; there is no solely automated selection or decision with legal or similarly significant effects. Registration does not guarantee selection or a promotional code.

Notice updates

Pre-launch version: 15 October 2026, revision 2. Michael Cini and the three-month registration retention period were confirmed by the owner. The approved notice will show an effective date and version when registration opens. Material purpose changes require updated information and, where needed, new consent.
