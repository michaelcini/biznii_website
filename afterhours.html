AfterHours

Call screening · Schedules · Auto-reply

What it does

Business calls on your schedule.

Set your working hours, choose who can still reach you, and let AfterHours handle eligible incoming calls locally on your Android device.

Flexible schedules

Configure operating hours or quiet periods to control when call screening is active.

Allowed callers

Keep selected callers or contacts able to reach you when the active schedule would otherwise screen the call.

Optional SMS replies

Send a configured automatic response for eligible screened calls when you choose to enable the feature.

Local processing

Core screening, schedules, caller matching and statistics are designed to operate on the device rather than through a BizNii backend.

Privacy controls

AfterHours is built around minimal permissions, local data handling and user-controlled notification visibility.

Activity & status

See useful local activity and status information so you know how your configured schedules are behaving.
