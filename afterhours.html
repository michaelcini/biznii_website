AfterHours

Call screening · Schedules · Auto-reply

Business calls on your schedule

Stay reachable when it matters. Switch off when it doesn't.

AfterHours is designed for business users who want practical control over calls outside their chosen working hours while keeping core processing on the Android device.

Flexible schedules

Choose when call screening is active using working-hour and quiet-period schedules.

Allowed callers

Let important contacts through even when an AfterHours schedule is active.

Optional SMS replies

Send a configured automatic response for eligible screened calls when you enable the feature.

Local processing

Core screening, matching, settings and statistics operate locally on your Android device.

Privacy-first design

Minimal permissions, local data handling and user-controlled notification visibility.

Activity & insights

See useful local activity and statistics so you understand how your schedules are behaving.

Privacy policy: the official AfterHours privacy page remains at the existing Google Play address: afterhours-privacy.html.
