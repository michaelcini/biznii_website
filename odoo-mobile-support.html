01 / GET CONNECTED

Connect to Odoo.

You’ll need access to an Odoo 19 server and an Odoo user with the appropriate permissions. If your organisation manages your connection, ask its administrator for the details.

  1. Create a named connection profile.
  2. Enter your HTTPS server address and exact database name.
  3. Choose an Odoo API key or username and password.
  4. Run the connection test, then save the profile.
  5. Select the company you want to work with.

Use your organisation’s approved account. Keep passwords and API keys private.

02 / YOUR ACCESS

Find the right tools.

Available tools follow your Odoo modules, company and user permissions. Your administrator can grant the Contacts, Sales, Inventory, Purchasing or Accounting access you need.

If a tool is missing, check your selected connection and company before asking your administrator to review your permissions.

03 / WORK WITHOUT A SIGNAL

Keep pending work safe.

Cached customers are available to read offline. To Dos and supported job photos, signatures and documents can wait in an encrypted queue for your next connection.

Reconnect and review pending items or conflicts in Administration. Sales, stock, purchasing, accounting actions and private-chat sending require an online connection.

Sync or review pending changes before clearing app storage or uninstalling.

04 / PRIVACY & DEVICE CONTROLS

Choose what’s visible.

Manage notification permissions in Android settings. Use the app’s privacy screen and sensitive-section locks where needed, and review lock-screen and home-screen widget visibility.

Connections and credentials belong to the device profile. Removing a saved connection does not delete the Odoo account or the organisation’s records.

QUICK ANSWERS

Get back
to your day.

Start with these checks. If you still need help, send us the app version, Android version and a short description of what happened.

My connection test fails.

Confirm that your server opens in a browser, its HTTPS certificate is valid, the database name is exact and your credentials are current. For a managed server, ask your administrator whether your Odoo version and permissions are supported.

I can’t see a record or a workspace.

Check the selected company and connection. Odoo access rules still apply, and optional modules or fields may not be available on your server.

A change is waiting to sync.

Reconnect to the same Odoo profile and review queue health or conflict details in Administration. Keep the pending work on the device until you have confirmed the result.

Notifications arrive later than expected.

Check Android notification and alarm permissions, battery restrictions and the app’s reminder settings. Background checks can be delayed by Android or device restrictions.

How do I remove data from my phone?

Delete a saved connection to remove its profile and credential. Supported cache clearing is available in Administration. To remove all app-private device data, use Android’s Clear storage option or uninstall. This does not delete records held on the Odoo server.
