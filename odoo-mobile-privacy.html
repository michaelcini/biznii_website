Who we are

This privacy policy applies to BizNii Odoo Mobile, the Android app provided by BizNii. For privacy enquiries, contact support@biznii.com.

The app connects to an existing Odoo account on the server you configure. It does not create a separate BizNii cloud account or operate a separate BizNii business-record or messaging server. Your organisation or Odoo service provider controls the records, user access, hosting and retention on that server. Its privacy policy also applies to those records.

Information we handle

The information available to the app depends on your organisation’s Odoo modules, your user permissions and the features you use.

Information processed by BizNii Odoo Mobile
InformationPurpose & destination
Connection and account detailsServer address, database name, login, user identifiers, selected company and API key or password are used to authenticate with your chosen Odoo server. Saved credentials are encrypted locally.
Business and contact recordsCustomer and supplier names, addresses, email addresses and telephone numbers; products, tasks, calendar items, sales, purchases, invoices, payments and accounting records are accessed and updated on Odoo for the workflows you use. Some data is cached on the device.

The Odoo server and its hosting provider receive network requests and may record connection information, such as IP address, request times and activity, under their own policies.

How information is used

  • Authenticate your connection and apply your Odoo company and access rights.
  • Display, create and update supported business records, messages and files at your direction.
  • Provide search, recent records, favourites, calendars, widgets and reminders.
  • Keep permitted cached data available and retry supported pending changes.
  • Diagnose problems and respond to support requests you send.

The current app does not include advertising or sell business data. It does not include a BizNii advertising analytics or automatic remote crash-reporting service. The Google scanning component has its own service diagnostics, described below.

Sharing and third-party services

Your Odoo server: credentials, record requests, messages and selected uploads go to the server you configure. The organisation’s authorised users and providers may access them according to Odoo permissions and its policies.

Android apps you choose: calling, email, maps, opening files and sharing reports invoke system applications or an Android share sheet. Selected recipients and apps receive the information needed for that action. Map actions pass business addresses or route stops; the app does not request device location permission or track your background location.

Camera and file selection: Android’s camera application and system file picker let you capture or select the files you choose. Selected content can be attached to Odoo records or messages; the app does not upload your entire photo library or address book.

Google code scanner: barcode image processing takes place on the device; Google states that it does not store scanner images or scan results. Google Play services/ML Kit may collect device and app information, device or installation identifiers, performance and usage metrics, errors and scanning configuration, including auto-zoom session metrics, to operate, diagnose and improve the service. See Google’s code scanner information, ML Kit data disclosures and Google’s privacy policy.

Support: information you email is handled by BizNii and the email providers involved. Share only what is needed to resolve the enquiry. Do not send credentials or unnecessary personal or confidential business records.

The location of your organisation’s Odoo hosting, your chosen recipients and external service providers determines where information is processed. Review those providers’ policies for their processing locations and safeguards.

Security and device protection

Normal remote Odoo connections use HTTPS. Saved credentials are encrypted using keys protected by Android Keystore. Business caches and retry databases are encrypted, and connection profiles keep separate data contexts. App-private business databases and profile metadata are excluded from Android backup and device transfer.

You can enable the privacy screen to block app screenshots and recent-app previews, and use device authentication for supported sensitive sections. Authentication is handled by Android; BizNii Odoo Mobile does not receive your biometric templates. Widgets, notifications and exported files can be visible outside the app, so review their privacy settings and recipients.

Protect your phone and Odoo credentials. Once you export or share a file, the receiving app, person or storage service controls its copy.

Retention and deletion

On your device: saved connection details remain until you remove them or clear app storage. Cached records, diagnostics and pending work remain as needed for app operation until cleared, replaced or removed. Synced queue entries are resolved according to the app’s sync process. Deleting a connection removes its saved profile and credential; it does not guarantee removal of every cached file or pending change.

Use Administration to clear supported caches. This preserves credentials and pending work. To remove all app-private device data, use Android Settings → Apps → BizNii Odoo Mobile → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Sync or review pending changes first: clearing storage can permanently remove work that has not reached Odoo. Files you exported to other folders or apps must be deleted separately.

On Odoo: uninstalling the app or deleting a connection does not delete your Odoo account, messages, attachments or business records. Ask your organisation’s Odoo administrator for access, correction or deletion requests. Its retention requirements, including accounting or legal obligations, apply to server records and backups.

Support correspondence: email messages and attachments are retained to handle your enquiry and related support follow-up. You may request deletion at support@biznii.com; necessary records may be retained where required for legal obligations or resolving a dispute. External providers apply their own retention policies to data they hold.

Your choices and rights

You choose the Odoo server and the records or files you act on. You can remove connection profiles, clear app-private storage and manage notifications, alarm access, device locks and app permissions in Android settings.

BizNii Odoo Mobile uses your existing Odoo account; it does not offer new user-account registration. Requests to delete or change that account belong with the organisation that manages it. For information held by BizNii through support, email us to request access, correction or deletion. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy of your information, or raise a concern with your data-protection authority.

Children

BizNii Odoo Mobile is designed for business use and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to BizNii through support, contact us so we can review and address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected in the app’s privacy information and store disclosures where appropriate.
