05 / TEAM, WIDGETS & REMINDERS

Keep the conversation
with the work.

Discuss, wherever work takes you

Read and send messages in supported private conversations. Share attachments, follow Inbox and Mentions, and open a record’s discussion from the work itself. Supported password sessions receive live updates, with periodic checks as a fallback.

Your Android home screen

An adaptive full-month activity widget includes task colours, completed work, overdue styling, pending summaries and date shortcuts. The Discuss widget brings All, Inbox and Mentions to the home screen, live updates on supported connections, and shortcuts to conversations and one-to-one calls.

Reminders with context

Opt into appointment warnings, due-today reminders and overdue digests every 6 or 12 hours. Use Settings → Notifications → Show overdue summary now to request an expandable summary from the current workspace’s saved tasks. Low-stock and overdue-invoice alerts are also optional. Appointment reminders follow your Android notification and alarm settings. Expand an overdue notification to see available task details; Android may initially show only the count. Business alerts show counts, and lock-screen public content is redacted.

Messages and calls require a connection. Background listening is optional and needs notifications and Android permission to keep running; device restrictions can delay or prevent delivery.

DISCUSS / ONE-TO-ONE CALLS

A quick question.
A familiar voice.

Move from a private chat to a voice or video call. Connect with another BizNii user, or a colleague in standard Odoo Discuss on a supported PC browser configuration.

Answer straight from the ring

See who is calling. Answer a video invitation with video or choose audio only. With background listening enabled and Android delivery permitted, incoming calls can reach you outside the chat and on the lock screen.

Keep the controls in reach

Mute, choose speaker output, switch cameras or turn video off. Remote video and your small local preview stay clear of the controls; connection status appears beneath the call identity.

Your app, your appearance

Choose Light, Dark or System, with optional Android dynamic colours. The same call surface follows your appearance when opened from the foreground or a background invitation.

One-to-one calls require a supported regular Odoo 16–20 release and username/password sign-in. Odoo 14/15, API-key connections and unsupported rolling Online builds do not support calling. 09 / BEFORE YOU CONNECT

A clear view of what’s supported.

What works offline?

Open cached customers, tasks and appointments. Queue supported To-Do creation, edits, completion/reopening, scheduling and deletion, plus job photos, signatures, documents and supported calendar moves. Reconnect to reconcile pending work and review conflicts. Sales, stock, purchasing, accounting and private-message sending require an online connection.

Which Odoo and Android versions?

Android 8.0 or newer, with supported Odoo 14–20 business workflows. Availability depends on Odoo version and edition, installed apps, user permissions and hosting entitlement. Use username/password with Odoo Online and Odoo.sh, including the Odoo two-factor prompt when requested. API keys serve supported self-hosted business workflows. Password sign-in and a successful connection test or trial do not establish contractual entitlement on every Odoo Online plan; check with your provider.

Which financial reports are available?

Ageing shows balances as of today. App-generated profit-and-loss, bank, cash and VAT summaries support financial years ending 31 December. Check them against your Odoo accounts. Balance Sheet and statutory reports open in Odoo Accounting.

How are stock and product operations handled?

Review stock movements before confirming them. If a transfer is interrupted or needs attention, check its status in Odoo Inventory before continuing. Manage multi-variant product editing in Odoo. Kit/BOM tools require the relevant Odoo functionality and permissions; they are not a complete manufacturing execution system. Local warranty certificates are separate from official Odoo documents.

How do live messages and background calls arrive?

Supported password-session connections receive live Discuss updates, with periodic checks as a fallback. Opt into the background listener in Settings and allow notifications and Android permission for it to run. A quiet listener notification is required. Battery restrictions, force-stop and process termination can prevent delivery. This is an on-device listener, not a guaranteed server-push service.

Which calls and networks are supported?

Calls are one-to-one on supported regular Odoo 16–20 releases with username/password. They are unavailable on 14/15, API-key connections and unsupported rolling Online builds. Microphone permission is needed for voice; camera permission is needed when using video. Wi-Fi or mobile data must provide a working path through Odoo’s configured calling services. Some networks need a relay; changing networks can interrupt a call. A media-status throttling warning can appear without hanging up media that is still flowing.

How do the trial and lifetime purchase work?

Start an optional 72-hour trial after connecting to Odoo. It does not automatically charge you. Lifetime access is a separate one-time Google Play purchase, with no subscription. Google Play shows the price and applicable taxes before confirmation. Restore uses the Play account associated with this installation. Promo redemption and ownership updates can take time; return to the app and use Restore purchase. Pending purchases do not grant lifetime access. Odoo hosting and third-party services are separate.

Is BizNii an official Odoo app?

BizNii for Odoo is an independent native Android client and is not affiliated with Odoo S.A. Your records stay in Odoo. No additional BizNii server module is required. Your Odoo hosting plan must allow the required external API access; check this with your provider. Odoo is a trademark of Odoo S.A.
