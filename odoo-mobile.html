Odoo Mobile

Built for everyday Odoo work

Fast access to the workflows that matter.

Odoo stays your system of record. The app focuses on quick mobile access for field work, customers, documents, tasks and internal collaboration.

Customers & vendors

Search contacts quickly and keep call, email and location actions close at hand.

Quotes & invoices

Work with sales documents from a phone-first interface designed for fast everyday use.

Calendar & To-Do

See appointments, tasks and schedule information without digging through desktop-style screens.

Internal collaboration

Keep discussions connected to Odoo records so work context stays in one place.

Offline-ready
