Switch between named Odoo connections and companies. Open cached work quickly. Keep credentials, tasks and queued changes protected on the device.

01 / OFFLINE

Useful without a signal.

Cached customers stay readable. To Do creates, edits, scheduling, completion, reopening, deletion and job evidence use persistent encrypted retry queues. Recovery adopts committed server changes after uncertain responses and flags conflicts for review.

02 / CONNECTIONS

The right business context.

Use an API key or username/password. Test before saving a profile. Select a company per connection; permissions, caches, recent records, favourites and queued tasks keep their profile and company context.

03 / SECURITY

Protection you control.

Android Keystore protects AES-GCM credentials and encryption keys. SQLCipher encrypts business caches and outboxes. HTTPS, optional private CA roots and certificate pins, biometric section locks and screenshot privacy controls add protection.

04 / ADMINISTRATION

Know what’s happening.

Inspect effective read/create/edit permissions, local cache and queue health, conflicts and sync timings. Review privacy-safe diagnostics on the device, share them explicitly, and clear supported local caches without deleting Odoo records.

09 / BEFORE YOU CONNECT

A clear view of what’s supported.

What works offline?

Previously cached customers are read-only offline. To Dos and supported job evidence can queue changes for retry, with conflict review and recovery after interrupted responses. Sales, inventory, purchasing, accounting actions and private-chat sending need an online connection. Other business-record file operations require connectivity.

Which Odoo and Android versions?

Android 8.0 / API 26 or newer. The app uses Odoo 19 JSON-2, including compatible rolling Odoo Online/Odoo.sh 19 versions checked through an actual compatibility request. Connect with an API key or username/password. Optional modules and effective permissions are discovered at runtime.

What are the accounting report boundaries?

Ageing uses the current date. Local P&L, bank/cash and VAT summaries require a calendar-year fiscal period ending 31 December and carry reconciliation warnings. Balance Sheet and configured or localized statutory reporting open in native Odoo Accounting. Customer statements use complete posted journal-entry datasets.

How are stock and product operations handled?

Supported transfers use separately committed, journaled stages and reconciliation. Unsupported or conflicting operations require native Odoo Inventory/Barcode review. Multi-variant product editing stays in Odoo. Stock operations are not one atomic transaction.

Are notifications instant?

Discussion background checks have a nominal 15-minute interval. Android and device restrictions can delay them. Appointment alarms depend on Android permission. Private chat is online-only; fully durable offline file composition and real-time killed-app push remain deferred.

Is BizNii an official Odoo app?

BizNii Odoo Mobile is an independent native Android client. Odoo remains the business system of record. No custom Odoo server module or separate messaging backend is required. Odoo is a trademark of Odoo S.A.
